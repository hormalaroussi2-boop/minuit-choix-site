Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how Minuit Choix Publisher ("the app", "we") handles information when the creator of @minuit.choix connects a TikTok account to it.

1. Who can use the app

The app is a private publishing tool operated by Minuit Choix for its own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

2. Information we access

  • Basic TikTok profile info (open ID, display name, avatar) via user.info.basic, only to show which account will publish.
  • Creator info returned by the Content Posting API (allowed privacy levels, interaction settings, max video duration), only to show the posting options.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, needed to publish on the creator's behalf.

We do not access followers, messages, contacts, viewing history, location or any other TikTok data.

3. How we use it

Solely to upload videos produced by Minuit Choix, with the caption and settings chosen by the creator, to the authorizing account (scope video.publish / video.upload). No advertising, profiling, analytics resale or AI training.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored encrypted in a secured server environment, never in public files or code, and are only sent to TikTok's API (open.tiktokapis.com). Profile info is not stored beyond the publishing session.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties. Videos are sent only to TikTok.

6. Retention and deletion

You can revoke access at any time in TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions. Tokens are deleted on revocation or on request by email within 30 days.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

You may request access, correction or deletion of your data, or object to processing, by emailing us. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (e.g. the Belgian APD/GBA).

8. Children

The app is not intended for people under 18.

9. Changes

Updates will be posted on this page with a new date.

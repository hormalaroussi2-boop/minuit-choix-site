Minuit Choix Publisher

Minuit Choix Publisher is a private tool used by the creator of @minuit.choix to publish its own interactive horror short videos to its own TikTok account.

What it does
  • The creator signs in with TikTok (Login Kit) and authorizes the app.
  • The tool shows the creator's TikTok nickname, lets the creator choose the privacy level, allow or disable comments, Duet and Stitch, and disclose AI-generated / branded content.
  • After the creator confirms, the finished video and its caption are uploaded with the TikTok Content Posting API.

The app only posts videos created by Minuit Choix to the account that authorized it. It does not read, collect or post anything else.
